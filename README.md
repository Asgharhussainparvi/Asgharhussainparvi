## About

I’m an **AI & Automation Engineer** and Software Engineering student who likes turning ambitious ideas and repetitive work into practical, testable software. I work across **agentic AI, language-model applications, backend APIs, computer vision, and workflow automation**—with an emphasis on systems that are useful, transparent, and grounded in evidence.

My path has moved from machine-learning and computer-vision projects into full-stack AI applications and agent workflows. These days, I’m especially interested in the engineering around AI: connecting tools and services, making outputs verifiable, and building responsible systems that communicate their limits.

> **The goal:** make intelligent software work in the real world—not just in a demo.

**Currently building and exploring:** reliable AI agents, evidence-grounded applications, API-first products, and automation that saves people time.
